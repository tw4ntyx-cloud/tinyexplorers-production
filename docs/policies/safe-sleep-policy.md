        # Safe Sleep Policy

        ## Summary

        This document is part of the parent information and policy set for Tiny Explorers Nursery and Preschool.

        ## Policy Content

        ### Safe Sleep Environment

Infants will sleep in designated cribs that meet safety standards. Cribs are equipped with firm mattresses and fitted sheets with no loose bedding.

### Monitoring During Sleep

While children are sleeping, staff will perform regular checks at least every 15 minutes and ensure the child remains comfortable and safe.

### Parent Communication and Education

Parents will be informed of the Safe Sleep Policy upon enrollment and encouraged to discuss any specific sleep needs or preferences for their child.
