        # Discipline Policy

        ## Summary

        This document is part of the parent information and policy set for Tiny Explorers Nursery and Preschool.

        ## Policy Content

        ### Purpose

At Tiny Explorers Nursery and Preschool, we believe children learn best when they feel safe, respected, and supported. Our discipline practices guide behavior in a calm, consistent, and developmentally appropriate way.

### Guiding Principles

Adults model calm responses and clear boundaries. Children are supported in understanding expectations, repairing harm, and learning new skills. Our approach is restorative rather than punitive.

### Implementation

When behavior needs support, educators respond with warmth, clarity, and consistency. Parents are informed of significant concerns and invited to work with staff to provide a consistent approach at home and at school.
