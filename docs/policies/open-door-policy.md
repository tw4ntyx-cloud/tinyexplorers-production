        # Open-Door Policy for Parents and Guardians

        ## Summary

        This document is part of the parent information and policy set for Tiny Explorers Nursery and Preschool.

        ## Policy Content

        ### Our Commitment

We believe that a strong partnership between our staff and families is essential for the well-being and development of our children. To foster transparency, trust, and open communication, we have implemented this Open-Door Policy.

### Access to the Classroom

Parents and guardians are welcome to visit classrooms during regular school hours and are encouraged to observe, participate, and connect with staff.

### Communication with Teachers and Staff

Teachers and staff are available to discuss progress, address concerns, and answer questions. Scheduled meetings can also be arranged for more in-depth conversation.

### Transparency and Trust

We believe transparency in practice builds trust and confidence in the care and education we provide.
