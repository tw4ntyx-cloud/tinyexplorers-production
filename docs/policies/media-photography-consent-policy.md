        # Media and Photography Consent Policy

        ## Summary

        This document is part of the parent information and policy set for Tiny Explorers Nursery and Preschool.

        ## Policy Content

        ### Purpose

At Tiny Explorers Nursery and Preschool, we recognize that media and photography are valuable tools for documenting children’s development, celebrating achievements, and sharing our activities with families.

### Consent Process

Parents or guardians will be provided with a Media and Photography Consent Form upon enrollment. Consent preferences may be updated at any time by contacting the school.

### Use of Media

Photographs and videos may be used for documenting educational activities, celebrating achievements, and supporting individual learning plans, subject to family consent.

### Protection and Privacy

Media and photography materials will be handled with respect for privacy and confidentiality. The school will protect the identity of children and families where consent does not permit wider sharing.
