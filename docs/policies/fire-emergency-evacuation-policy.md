        # Fire and Emergency Evacuation Plan Policy

        ## Summary

        This document is part of the parent information and policy set for Tiny Explorers Nursery and Preschool.

        ## Policy Content

        ### Preparedness

Tiny Explorers maintains clear plans for fire, evacuation, and other emergency situations. Staff are trained to respond promptly, calmly, and in a way that protects the children in their care.

### Response and Communication

In the event of an emergency, staff will guide children to safety, account for each child, and communicate with families as appropriate.
