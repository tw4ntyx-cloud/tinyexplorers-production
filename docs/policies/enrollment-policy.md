        # Enrollment Policy

        ## Summary

        This document is part of the parent information and policy set for Tiny Explorers Nursery and Preschool.

        ## Policy Content

        ### Admissions Process

Enrollment begins with an inquiry, followed by a tour and a conversation with our admissions team. If a place is available and suitable, families are invited to complete the application process and confirm placement.

### Documentation and Readiness

Families are asked to provide relevant medical, emergency, and enrollment information so we can prepare for a calm and well-supported start. We support a phased transition period for new children.
