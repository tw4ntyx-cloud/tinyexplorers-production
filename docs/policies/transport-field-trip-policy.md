        # Transport and Field Trip Policy

        ## Summary

        This document is part of the parent information and policy set for Tiny Explorers Nursery and Preschool.

        ## Policy Content

        ### Transportation Guidelines

All children will be transported in vehicles that meet all safety standards and regulations, with age-appropriate car seats or seat belts as required by law.

### Field Trip Guidelines

Parents will be informed in advance about upcoming field trips, including the destination, date, time, and any special instructions. Close supervision and clear safety procedures will be maintained throughout each trip.
