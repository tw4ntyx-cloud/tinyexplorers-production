        # Mandatory Reporter Policy

        ## Summary

        This document is part of the parent information and policy set for Tiny Explorers Nursery and Preschool.

        ## Policy Content

        ### Duty to Report

All staff members are expected to act in the best interests of children and to report any concerns about welfare, abuse, or neglect in accordance with legal and ethical responsibilities.

### Management of Concerns

Reports are made to the designated safeguarding lead and handled in a serious, professional, and timely way. The welfare of the child remains the highest priority.
