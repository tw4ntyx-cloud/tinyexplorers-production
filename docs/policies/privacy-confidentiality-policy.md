        # Privacy and Confidentiality Policy

        ## Summary

        This document is part of the parent information and policy set for Tiny Explorers Nursery and Preschool.

        ## Policy Content

        ### Purpose

We are dedicated to protecting the privacy and confidentiality of all individuals associated with our program, including children, parents, guardians, and staff.

### Collection of Personal Information

We collect personal information necessary for the effective operation of our nursery and preschool, including health and emergency contact details, enrollment and attendance records, and staff records.

### Use and Access to Personal Information

Personal information will be used only for the purposes for which it was collected and accessed only by authorized personnel.
