        # Media Viewing Policy

        ## Summary

        This document is part of the parent information and policy set for Tiny Explorers Nursery and Preschool.

        ## Policy Content

        ### Purpose

The use of media at Tiny Explorers is limited to content that is age-appropriate, educational, and consistent with our curriculum goals. We aim to minimize screen time and preserve space for interactive, hands-on learning.

### Media Content Guidelines

Media content must be suitable for the age and developmental stage of the children. Media used in the classroom should have clear educational objectives and align with our curriculum.

### Media Viewing Procedures

Screen time for children will be limited and supervised by a staff member at all times. Media viewing will be scheduled as part of a balanced daily routine.
