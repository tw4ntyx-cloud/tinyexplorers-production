        # Medicine Policy

        ## Summary

        This document is part of the parent information and policy set for Tiny Explorers Nursery and Preschool.

        ## Policy Content

        ### General Guidelines

We prioritize the health and safety of all children. Medication will only be administered with written consent from a parent or guardian and must be stored securely.

### Administration of Medication

Only trained staff may administer medication. Each administration is recorded in a medication log and parents are notified of any concerns or missed doses.

### Special Considerations

Emergency medications will be kept in an easily accessible but secure location. Children with chronic conditions will have an individualized care plan where appropriate.
