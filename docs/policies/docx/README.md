# Editable policy copies (DOCX)

Complete Word versions of all twelve parent policies, generated with `frontend/scripts/policy-docs/` from the website's policy text (`frontend/src/data/policies.js`).

- **Eleven policies (POL-001 to POL-009, POL-011, POL-012):** editable copies of the published v1.0 PDFs, approved by the Board of Governors on 1 Sep 2026. The text matches the PDFs word for word. They are marked "Editable copy of the published PDF".
- **Privacy & Confidentiality (POL-010):** v1.1. Adds the Newsletter Sign-Up section and the six-month enquiry retention sentence, both approved by the owner on 19 Oct 2026. **Sign-off is pending:** the signatory's name is still to be provided.

These are **not served by the website**. Only the PDFs in `frontend/public/policies-docs/` are public. Regenerate after any policy text change: see `frontend/scripts/policy-docs/README.md`.
